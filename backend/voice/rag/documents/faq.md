# Tatva Direct — Voice Commerce FAQ

## How do I search for products?
Say what material or product you need, for example: "Find cement bags" or "Search for steel rods".

## How do I add to cart?
After search results, say "Add product" with the quantity, or provide the product name.

## How do I checkout?
Say "Checkout" or "Place my order". You will be asked to confirm before the order is created.

## Payment methods
Supported methods include cash on delivery (COD), online payment via Razorpay, bank transfer, and credit line where enabled.

## How do I track an order?
Say "Track order" followed by your order number.

## How do I cancel an order?
Say "Cancel order" and your order number. Cancellation is only allowed before shipping for most orders.

## Returns and refunds
Initiate returns from your orders page or ask about return policy. Refunds follow supplier and platform policy after goods are received.
