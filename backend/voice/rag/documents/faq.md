# Tatva Direct — Voice Commerce FAQ

## How do I search for products?
Say what material or product you need, for example: "Find cement bags" or "Search for steel rods". Results come from the live supplier catalog.

## How do I add to cart?
After search results, say "Add product" with the quantity, or provide the product name and how many units you need.

## How do I checkout?
Say "Checkout" or "Place my order". You will be asked to confirm before the order is created. Vendor selection may be automatic based on ranking.

## Payment methods
Supported methods include cash on delivery (COD), online payment via Razorpay, bank transfer, and credit line where enabled on your account.

## How do I pay online for an order?
Say "Pay online" with your order number. A Razorpay payment link or in-app flow may be started. Complete payment in the app if prompted.

## How do I track an order?
Say "Track order" followed by your order number. Tracking depends on logistics partner and whether a tracking link was generated at dispatch.

## How do I cancel an order?
Say "Cancel order" and your order number. Cancellation is only allowed before shipping for most orders. Give a short reason when asked.

## Returns and refunds overview
Initiate returns from your orders page or ask about return policy. Refunds follow supplier and platform policy after goods are received and the return is closed.

## Credit line and ledger
If your service provider has credit line enabled, eligible orders may be placed on credit subject to limits. Ledger and payment receipts are available in your account.

## Voice commands cheat sheet
Search products, add to cart, show cart, checkout, track order, cancel order, update shipping address, return policy, shipping policy, payment methods.
