# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery timelines
Delivery timelines depend on supplier location, logistics partner, and transport selection at checkout. Metro and nearby regions are usually faster than remote pin codes.

## Logistics partners
Courier booking may use integrated logistics (for example Shiprocket) when configured for your supplier and category. Trucking or bulk transport may apply for heavy materials.

## Tracking shipments
Track shipments using your order number or tracking link when available. Say "Track order" plus the order ID in voice.

## Shipping address
Shipping address is taken from your service provider profile. Update it by saying "Update my shipping address" and providing line1, city, state, and pincode.

## Partial shipments
Large orders may ship in multiple consignments. Each consignment may have its own tracking status when logistics is integrated.

## Delivery issues
For delayed, missing, or wrong deliveries, report with your order number and a short description. Damaged goods may qualify for return per returns policy.

## Delivery charges
Shipping or logistics charges may appear at checkout depending on supplier, weight, distance, and selected transport mode.
