# Payments and Billing

## Cash on delivery (COD)
COD is available where enabled. Pay the delivery partner or as instructed on the invoice when goods arrive.

## Online payment (Razorpay)
Online payment uses Razorpay for eligible orders. Say "Pay online" with your order ID in voice to start payment setup. Complete payment in the app if a link or checkout opens.

## Bank transfer
Bank transfer may be offered for certain suppliers or order types. Transfer details appear on the order or invoice when applicable.

## Credit line
Credit line lets eligible service providers place orders on account up to an approved limit. Settlement follows your platform ledger and due dates.

## Payment receipts
Payment receipts and invoices may be downloaded from your orders or payments section after payment is recorded.

## Failed or pending payments
If online payment fails, retry from the order page or use another enabled method. Pending payments may block dispatch until confirmed.

## Refunds to original method
Approved refunds are typically returned to the original payment method when possible. COD orders may be settled via bank transfer or platform credit per policy.
