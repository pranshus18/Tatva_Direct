# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Eligibility
Orders may be returned according to supplier acceptance and platform phase-2 returns policy. Eligibility depends on order status, product category, and time since delivery.

## How to request a return
Request a return from your order details while the order is eligible. In voice, say "return policy" or "how do refunds work" for guidance, then use the orders page to submit.

## Partial returns
Partial returns may be supported per line item quantity when the supplier allows splitting quantities on a purchase order line.

## Refund timing
Refunds are processed after the return is acknowledged, goods are received or inspected as required, and the return case is closed. Timing varies by payment method (COD vs online vs credit line).

## Damaged or incorrect items
Damaged or incorrect items should be reported with order number and description. Photos may be requested through support channels. Do not discard goods until instructed if inspection is required.

## Non-returnable items
Some categories (custom cut, perishable, or special-order materials) may be non-returnable. Supplier terms on the product or order apply.

## Cancellation vs return
Cancelling an unshipped order is not the same as returning delivered goods. Cancel before dispatch; return after delivery when eligible.
